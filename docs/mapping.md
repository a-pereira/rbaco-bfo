# Pattern mapping: ROWLBAC to RBO

Each pattern in ROWLBAC (Finin et al., SACMAT 2008, pp.73–82) mapped to the RBO terms and queries that cover it, the matching element of the NIST standard (Ferraiolo et al., ACM TISSEC 4(3), 2001, pp.224–274), and whether ROWLBAC's version conforms to NIST. Section numbers are ROWLBAC's and page numbers are NIST's printed pages unless stated otherwise. Term numbers abbreviate RBO IRIs: `006` is `https://example.org/rbac-bfo/RBO_0000006`. `rbo-terms.csv` gives labels and definitions.

The scope is P1 to P18 plus the §3.1 scenario, as agreed during clarification. Left out, as agreed: §5 related work, the wireless-device domain of §4, and the appendix code.

## NIST elements no ROWLBAC pattern touches

Kept out by the "no more, no less" rule unless a pattern needed them: the administrative functions (`AddUser`, `DeleteUser`, `AddRole`, `DeleteRole`, `AssignUser`, `DeassignUser`, `GrantPermission`, `RevokePermission`, pp.242–246, 251–252) appear only as the dated acts `028`–`032`, which P14's state changes need. Review functions beyond authorized users, and the `CreateSsdSet`/`CreateDsdSet` administrative commands (pp.261, 267), have no term.

## Additions beyond ROWLBAC, each agreed during clarification

- Business process `004` and its plan `005`, standing in for NIST sessions.
- Whole-instance DSD counting `016` and plan scoping `115`.
- The dated policy acts `028`–`032` and role inheritance statements `017`.
- The code-review dataset (CR-42), the only example with several participants in one instance.

## Additions after Botha and Eloff (2001), agreed 2026-10-30

Tasks, work items, permission and task SoD, conflicting-agent designations, principals and the human performer requirement: classes `040`–`049` and properties `132`–`147`, with the purchase dataset (`data/purchase.ttl`). Botha and Eloff's patterns are mapped to RBO, with verdicts, in `docs/botha-review.md`; design-record D14 lists the choices. None touches a ROWLBAC pattern above, except that permission SoD widens the range of `113` (cites regulation) to SoD constraints and the domain of `115` (applies within) to designations and human performer requirements.

Added later the same day, after discussion: the task-bound operation requirement `050` and its property `148` (requires task binding for). It is not in Botha and Eloff, whose engine binds every WSession to a task, so it has no counterpart there. It widens `113`'s range and `115`'s domain once more, and makes `133` (requires operation) a condition of access for the operations it names.

Added 2026-10-31 with the corrections of design-record D15: `describes conflicting member` `149`, the parent of `126`, `146` and `147`. The conflict reports of P6, P7 and P9 still name role definitions with `126`, now a sub-property of it.
